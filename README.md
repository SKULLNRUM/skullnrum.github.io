# The Departure

The opening of the site is intentionally image-led. The supplied bird carrying a skull is the mark; do not add a large SKULLNRUM wordmark or title above it. Keep the black field clean and let the artwork carry the identity.

## Build direction
- Dark, atmospheric, underground pirate world.
- Artwork-led design, hidden details and unlockable content.
- Distinct responsive desktop and mobile composition.
- Build one section at a time.
- Preserve the user's original artwork; never replace it with unrelated pirate imagery.

## Current experience
1. **The Departure** — the exact supplied bird-and-skull image starts centered on a pure black field. Scrolling drives its flight diagonally toward the top-right while it shrinks into the distance and fades away. This is scroll-controlled, not an idle looping animation.
2. **The Bottle** — after the bird's departure, an illustrated antique rum bottle appears as the next focal point. Selecting it opens a sign-in / create-account panel.
3. The account panel is currently a front-end interaction only. Authentication, account storage and member data must be connected to a backend before real accounts can be created or used.

## Roadmap
1. The Departure — refine the scroll flight and transition.
2. The Bottle / The Wake — develop the rum bottle entrance and then the opening skull, ocean waves and ship.
3. The Crew — artist identity, member profiles, usernames, comments and live chat.
4. Restricted — gated unreleased-music room.
5. Admin — moderation and management controls.
6. Hidden details — secrets, easter eggs and unlockable content throughout the experience.

Build one section at a time. Keep the site atmospheric and immersive rather than turning it into a conventional artist template.

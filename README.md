# The Departure

The opening of the site is intentionally image-led. The supplied bird carrying a skull is the mark; do not add a large SKULLNRUM wordmark or title above it. Keep the black field clean and let the artwork carry the identity.

## Build direction
- Dark, atmospheric, underground pirate world.
- Artwork-led design, hidden details and unlockable content.
- Distinct responsive desktop and mobile composition.
- Build one section at a time.

## Roadmap
1. The Departure — supplied bird-and-skull artwork, then movement that creates a sense of flight through perspective.
2. The Wake — opening skull, rum bottle, ocean and ship.
3. The Crew — artist identity, accounts, usernames, comments and live chat.
4. Restricted — gated unreleased-music room.
5. Admin — moderation and management controls.

Use the exact artwork supplied by the user. Do not substitute unrelated pirate imagery.
